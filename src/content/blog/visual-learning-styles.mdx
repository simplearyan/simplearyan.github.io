When explaining complex topics, the *vibe* matters. A formal theorem might need a stark, high-contrast look, while a rough intuition pump works best with a hand-drawn, "napkin" aesthetic.

We've upgraded our tools to support this range.

Sometimes you just want the data to float on the page. Our new **Minimal** graph variant strips away the heavy borders and backgrounds.

Compare this to the "Paper" variant we used before. This one feels lighter, more modern, and integrates seamlessly into the text flow.

<Callout type="tip" title="Design Tip">
  Use **Minimal** graphs when you want the reader to focus purely on the geometry, without the distraction of a "frame".
</Callout>
