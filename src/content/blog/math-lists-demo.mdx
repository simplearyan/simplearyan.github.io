In educational content, structure is everything. We can't just use simple bullet points for everything. 
Here we demonstrate new list styles designed for **step-by-step solutions**, **quizzes**, and **fun notes**.

## 1. Step-by-Step Solutions
The `steps` variant is perfect for derivations or algorithms. Notice the connected vertical line.

### Problem: Solve for $x$ in $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$

<List variant="steps">
  <li>
    **Factor the Quadratic Equation**
    Find two numbers that multiply to $6$ and add to $-5$. These are $-2$ and $-3$.
    So, $(x-2)(x-3) = 0$.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Set Each Factor to Zero**
    Either $x - 2 = 0$ or $x - 3 = 0$.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Solve for x**
    $x = 2$ or $x = 3$.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Final Verification**
    Plug $2$ back in: $4 - 10 + 6 = 0$. Correct.
  </li>
</List>

## 2. Quiz / Multiple Choice
The `options` variant automatically labels items as A, B, C, D and provides a clickable card-like feel.

### Question: What is the derivative of $\sin(x)$?

<List variant="options">
  <li>$\cos(x)$</li>
  <li>$-\cos(x)$</li>
  <li>$\tan(x)$</li>
  <li>$\sec^2(x)$</li>
</List>

### Question: Which vector is orthogonal to $(1, 2)$?

<List variant="options">
  <li>$(2, 1)$</li>
  <li>$(-2, 1)$</li>
  <li>$(1, -2)$</li>
  <li>$(0, 0)$ (Trivial, but technically yes)</li>
</List>
