<CinematicText variant="comic-verse" as="h2" class="mb-6 mt-12" color="#3b82f6">1. Market Segmentation (Sets)</CinematicText>

In marketing, understanding the overlap between customer interests is key. A **Venn Diagram** visually computes probabilities like $P(A \cup B)$ (Union) or $P(A \cap B)$ (Intersection).

**Scenario:** A survey of 100 people asked if they own a **Laptop** or a **Tablet**.
*   Laptop Owners: 70
*   Tablet Owners: 50
*   Both: 30

<div class="flex justify-center my-8">
    <VennDiagram 
        type="2-set" 
        labels={["Laptop", "Tablet"]} 
        colors={["#3b82f6", "#10b981"]}
    />
</div>

**Insight**: The probability of finding someone who owns *only* a tablet is $P(Tablet) - P(Both) = 0.5 - 0.3 = 0.2$ (20%). The diagram makes this subtraction intuitive.

<CinematicText variant="comic-verse" as="h2" class="mb-6 mt-12" color="#ef4444">2. Correlation (Scatter Plots)</CinematicText>

Does warmer weather lead to more ice cream sales? A **Scatter Plot** reveals the relationship (correlation) between two quantitative variables.

**Data:** Average Daily Temperature (°C) vs. Ice Cream Sales ($).

<div class="flex justify-center my-8">
    <ScatterPlot 
        data={[
            {x: 14, y: 215}, {x: 16, y: 325}, {x: 11, y: 185}, {x: 19, y: 332},
            {x: 18, y: 406}, {x: 24, y: 522}, {x: 22, y: 412}, {x: 25, y: 614}
        ]}
        xLabel="Temp (°C)"
        yLabel="Sales ($)"
        color="#f97316"
    />
</div>

**Analysis:**
The red trendline shows a strong **positive correlation**. As temperature ($X$) rises, sales ($Y$) consistently increase. The slope of the line tells us exactly how many extra dollars we earn for each degree of warming.

<CinematicText variant="comic-verse" as="h2" class="mb-6 mt-12" color="#eab308">3. Rare Events (Combinatorics)</CinematicText>

In Poker, a "Royal Flush" is the rarest hand. Why? Because the number of specific combinations is tiny compared to the total number of 5-card hands ($\binom{52}{5} = 2,598,960$).

**The Royal Flush:**

<div class="flex justify-center my-8">
    <PlayingCardHand overlap={-40}>
        <PlayingCard rank="10" suit="spades" />
        <PlayingCard rank="J" suit="spades" />
        <PlayingCard rank="Q" suit="spades" />
        <PlayingCard rank="K" suit="spades" />
        <PlayingCard rank="A" suit="spades" />
    </PlayingCardHand>
</div>

**Probability:**
There are only 4 ways to get a Royal Flush (one per suit).
$$P(Royal Flush) = \frac{4}{2,598,960} \approx 0.00000154$$
You are roughly 4 times more likely to get struck by lightning than to overlap these cards in a single deal!
