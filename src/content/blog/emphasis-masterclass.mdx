When we read on paper, we circle things. We highlight them. We scribble notes. Let"s bring that fidelity to the web.

## The Circle

Use the <CircleEmphasis color="#ef4444" padding="0.2rem">Circle Emphasis</CircleEmphasis> to draw immediate attention. It supports different variants:

- **Rough**: <CircleEmphasis variant="rough" color="red">Look at this!</CircleEmphasis>
- **Double**: For <CircleEmphasis variant="double" color="blue">extra importance</CircleEmphasis>.
- **Single**: A cleaner <CircleEmphasis variant="single" color="green">selection</CircleEmphasis>.

## The Highlighter

Just like your favorite neon markers.
- <MarkerHighlight color="yellow">Standard Yellow</MarkerHighlight>
- <MarkerHighlight color="pink">Neon Pink</MarkerHighlight>
- <MarkerHighlight color="cyan">Electric Cyan</MarkerHighlight>
- <MarkerHighlight color="green">Bright Green</MarkerHighlight>

## The Stroke scale

Underlines can be subtle or bold.
- **Thin**: <StrokeUnderline thickness="2px" color="black" class="dark:text-white">A delicate note.</StrokeUnderline>
- **Thick**: <StrokeUnderline thickness="6px" color="#facc15">A bold claim!</StrokeUnderline>
