Sometimes the container gets in the way. We've introduced a **Minimalist** variant for our math graphs that removes the borders and backgrounds, letting the function curve speak for itself—perfect for clean, modern interfaces.

## Minimalist Variant

By setting `variant="minimal"`, you strip away the outer shell.

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 2rem; align-items: end;">
    <EquationGraph 
        function="sin"
        expression="Math.sin(x) * x"
        domain={[-10, 10]}
        range={[-8, 8]}
        variant="minimal"
        title="Damped Sine Wave"
        height={200}
        color="#F43F5E"
    />
    <EquationGraph 
        function="exp"
        domain={[-2, 2]}
        range={[-0.5, 4]}
        variant="minimal"
        title="Exponential Growth"
        height={200}
        color="#10B981"
    />
</div>

### Code Example

```jsx
<EquationGraph 
    variant="minimal"
    function="sin"
    title="Minimal Sine"
/>
```
