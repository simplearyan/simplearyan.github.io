# Making Content Pop

In the age of endless scrolling, <Highlight color="yellow">capturing attention is an art</Highlight>. We've added a suite of tools to help you do just that, inspired by the visual storytelling of Vox and high-quality periodicals.

## 1. The Highlight

You can now use a marker-pen style highlight to emphasize key phrases. It's not just a background color; it has a subtle gradient to mimic a real highlighter.

> "Design is not just what it looks like and feels like. Design is <Highlight color="pink">how it works</Highlight>."

Available colors: `yellow` (default), `green`, `pink`, `blue`.

## 2. Hand-Drawn Emphasis

Sometimes you want to circle something on a "digital paper" to draw the eye.

<div style="position: relative; display: inline-block; padding: 2rem; border: 1px dashed var(--color-border); margin: 2rem 0;">
    The suspect was last seen near the <Circle delay={500}>old warehouse</Circle>.
    <Arrow direction="left" color="red" class="absolute -right-10 top-0" />
</div>

These elements animate when they scroll into view, adding a dynamic feel to static text.

## 3. Squiggly Lines

For a softer emphasis or to indicate something "uncertain", use the <Underline color="red">squiggly underline</Underline>. It mimics a pen squiggle.

## 4. Marginalia

Use marginal notes to add context without breaking the flow.

<Aside type="note" title="Did you know?">
    Marginalia (or apostils) are marks made in the margins of a book or other document. They may be scribbles, comments, glosses, critiques, doodles, or illuminations.
</Aside>

<Aside type="warn" title="Usage Warning">
    Don't overuse these effects! If everything is highlighted, <Highlight>nothing is highlighted</Highlight>.
</Aside>

## 5. Visual Storytelling

Combine these elements to create "investigative board" layouts or annotate images.

<div style="position: relative; margin: 3rem 0; padding: 2rem; background: var(--color-code-bg); border-radius: 8px;">
    <h3>The "Red String" Board</h3>
    <p>
        Evidence A connects directly to <Circle delay={200}>Evidence B</Circle>. 
    </p>
    <div style="display: flex; justify-content: space-between; align-items: center; margin-top: 2rem;">
         <div style="padding: 1rem; border: 1px solid var(--color-border); background: var(--color-bg);">Suspect</div>
         <Arrow direction="right" width={100} color="red" />
         <div style="padding: 1rem; border: 1px solid var(--color-border); background: var(--color-bg);">Crime Scene</div>
    </div>
</div>

## Source Code

These components are built with Astro and inline SVGs. They use `IntersectionObserver` to trigger animations only when the reader sees them.

```astro
<Highlight color="yellow">Hello World</Highlight>
<Circle delay={300}>Target</Circle>
<Arrow direction="up" />
```

Enjoy creating more engaging content!
