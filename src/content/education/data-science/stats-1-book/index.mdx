**"S1_VOL1_DESCRIPTIVE_STATISTICS.pdf"** by Prashant Sharma is a comprehensive guide to the foundational concepts of statistics, data organisation, and probability-based counting. It is structured into nine distinct chapters that move from basic definitions to complex measures of association and combinatorics.

**Analogy for the Sources:**
Think of this textbook as a **Building Plan**. Chapters 1 and 2 are the **foundations and materials** (defining what statistics and data are). Chapters 3 and 4 are the **structural beams** (summarising individual data types). Chapter 5 is the **electrical and plumbing** (showing how different parts connect), and Chapters 6 through 9 are the **blueprints for future extensions** (the counting rules needed for more advanced probability).
