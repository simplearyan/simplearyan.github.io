Probability assumes the model is known. **Statistics** assumes the model is unknown and must be inferred from data.

## The Empirical Distribution
Assigns probability $1/n$ to each observed data point.

## Estimates
We use sample statistics to estimate population parameters:
*   **Sample Mean ($\bar{X}$)** estimates $\mu$.
*   **Sample Variance ($S^2$)** estimates $\sigma^2$.

<Concept title="Unbiased Estimators">
Both $\bar{X}$ and $S^2$ are **unbiased**: their expected values equal the true parameters they estimate.
</Concept>

## Visualization
*   **Histograms**: For continuous data.
*   **Q-Q Plots**: Compare sample quantiles to theoretical quantiles to checking distributional assumptions.
