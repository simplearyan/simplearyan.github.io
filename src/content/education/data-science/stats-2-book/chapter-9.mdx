## Estimation Methods
*   **Method of Moments (MOM)**: Equate sample moments to population moments.
*   **Maximum Likelihood (MLE)**: Find parameters that maximize the probability of the observed data.

## Confidence Intervals
Instead of a point estimate, we provide a range of plausible values.
*   $\sigma$ known: **z-interval**
*   $\sigma$ unknown: **t-interval**

## Hypothesis Testing
<Concept title="The Process">
1.  **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$)**: The default position (e.g., "no effect").
2.  **P-value**: Probability of seeing data this extreme if $H_0$ is true.
3.  **Decision**: Reject $H_0$ if P-value $< \alpha$ (significance level).
</Concept>

Common tests include **z-test**, **t-test**, $\chi^2$-test, and **F-test**.
