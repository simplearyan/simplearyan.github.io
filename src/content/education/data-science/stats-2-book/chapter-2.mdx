## Bernoulli Trials

A **Bernoulli Trial** is an experiment with exactly two outcomes: **Success** or **Failure**.

<Concept title="Key Distributions">
1.  **Binomial Distribution**: The number of successes in $n$ independent Bernoulli trials.
2.  **Geometric Distribution**: The number of trials needed to get the *first* success.
</Concept>

## Poisson Approximation

When $n$ is large and $p$ is small, the Binomial distribution can be difficult to calculate. The **Poisson distribution** serves as a powerful limit approximation.

$$P(X=k) \approx e^{-\lambda} \frac{\lambda^k}{k!}$$

where $\lambda = np$.

## Sampling With and Without Replacement

*   **With Replacement**: Items are returned to the pool. Independence is maintained.
*   **Without Replacement**: Items are kept. Probabilities change. Described by the **Hypergeometric distribution**.

> Note: If the population is large compared to the sample size, the difference is negligible.
