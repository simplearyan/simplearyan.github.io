A **Random Variable** ($X$) is a function that maps outcomes to numerical values.

## Probability Mass Function (PMF)

For discrete variables, the distribution is defined by the PMF:

$$f_X(t) = P(X=t)$$

## Independence and Dependence

We extend independence to variables. A sequence is **i.i.d.** if the variables are:
1.  **Independent**
2.  **Identically Distributed**

<Concept title="Memoryless Property">
The **Geometric distribution** is memoryless. The probability of success in the next trial doesn't depend on how many failures have already occurred.
$$P(X > n+m | X > n) = P(X > m)$$
</Concept>
