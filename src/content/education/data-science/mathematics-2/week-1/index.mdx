## What is a Linear Equation?

A linear equation describes a straight line (in 2D) or a plane (in 3D).

<CinematicCard theme="paper" title="Definition">
  <p>A <strong>linear equation</strong> in $n$ variables $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$ is an equation of the form:</p>
  <p class="text-center text-xl my-4">$a_1x_1 + a_2x_2 + \dots + a_nx_n = b$</p>
  <div class="scribble-highlight mt-4 inline-block">Key Concept</div>
</CinematicCard>

### Systems of Equations

A system of linear equations is a collection of one or more linear equations involving the same variables.

<HandDrawnBox title="Example System" variant="tip">
  $$
  \begin{cases}
  2x + 3y = 5 \\
  4x - y = 3
  \end{cases}
  $$
</HandDrawnBox>
