A linear equation is an algebraic equation in which each term is either a constant or the product of a constant and a single variable.

## Solutions

A solution to a linear system is a sequence of numbers $(s_1, s_2, \dots, s_n)$ that satisfies every equation in the system.

### Types of Solutions

1.  **Unique Solution:** The lines intersect at exactly one point.
2.  **No Solution:** The lines are parallel and never intersect.
3.  **Infinitely Many Solutions:** The lines are identical (coincident).
