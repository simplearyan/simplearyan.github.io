## Usage
These problems are for self-practice and do not count towards your final grade.

## Problems

1.  Graph the following lines and find their intersection point:
    $y = 2x + 1$ and $y = -x + 4$

2.  Convert the following system into an augmented matrix:
    $$
    3x - 2y + z = 0 \\
    x + y - z = 5 \\
    -2x + 4y = 7
    $$
