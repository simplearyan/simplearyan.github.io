## The Eigenvalue Problem

For a square matrix $A$, we want to find non-zero vectors $v$ and scalars $\lambda$ such that:

<HandDrawnBox title="Equation" variant="tip">
  $$ Av = \lambda v $$
</HandDrawnBox>

Here, $\lambda$ is the **eigenvalue** and $v$ is the **eigenvector**.
