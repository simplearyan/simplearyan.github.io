## What is a Vector Space?

A vector space is a collection of objects called vectors, which may be added together and multiplied by numbers, called scalars.

<HandDrawnBox title="Definition" variant="tip">
  A set $V$ is a vector space if it satisfies 10 axioms, including:
  1. Closure under addition
  2. Closure under scalar multiplication
  3. Existence of zero vector
</HandDrawnBox>
