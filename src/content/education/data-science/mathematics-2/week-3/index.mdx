## Matrix Operations

Matrices are rectangular arrays of numbers. They are fundamental to linear algebra.

<HandDrawnBox title="Matrix Multiplication" variant="tip">
  If $A$ is an $m \times n$ matrix and $B$ is an $n \times p$ matrix, their product $AB$ is an $m \times p$ matrix.
</HandDrawnBox>
