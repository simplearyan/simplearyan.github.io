# Analysis of Variance (ANOVA)

Suppose we want to compare the effectiveness of **3 different diets** (A, B, and C).

If we used t-tests, we'd have to compare A vs B, B vs C, and A vs C. This increases the chance of a Type I error (finding a difference when there isn't one).

## The F-Statistic

ANOVA compares the variance **between** the groups to the variance **within** the groups.

<AnimatedCard animation="flip-up" variant="paper">
  $$ F = \frac{\text{Variance Between Groups}}{\text{Variance Within Groups}} $$
</AnimatedCard>

## Interpreting F

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 1rem;">
  <ScribbleCard scribbleStyle="neat" backPattern="diagonal">
    <div slot="front">
      ### Large F
      
      The groups are very different from each other compared to the noise within them.
    </div>
    <div slot="back">
      **Result**
      
      Reject $H_0$. At least one group mean is different.
    </div>
  </ScribbleCard>

  <ScribbleCard scribbleStyle="rough" backPattern="dots">
    <div slot="front">
      ### Small F
      
      The differences between groups are likely just random noise.
    </div>
    <div slot="back">
      **Result**
      
      Fail to Reject $H_0$. No significant difference.
    </div>
  </ScribbleCard>
</div>
