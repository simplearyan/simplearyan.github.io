To talk about "length" or "angle" in abstract spaces, we define the <StrokeUnderline variant="double" color="#06b6d4">Inner Product</StrokeUnderline>.

## Core Concepts
1.  **Inner Product**: Generalization of the dot product $\langle u, v \rangle$.
2.  **Norm (Length)**: Induced by the inner product $||\mathbf{v}|| = \sqrt{\langle \mathbf{v}, \mathbf{v} \rangle}$.
3.  **Orthogonality**: Two vectors are orthogonal if their inner product is **zero**.

<Concept title="Gram-Schmidt Orthonormalization">
  An algorithm to convert any basis into an **Orthonormal Basis** (orthogonal unit vectors).
  It works by sequentially subtracting the "projection" of the new vector onto the existing ones.
</Concept>

## Orthogonal Transformations
Transformations that preserve the inner product (and thus lengths and angles).
*   **Rotation Matrices** are prime examples.
